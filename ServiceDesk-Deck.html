How it works

From walk-in to collection — fully tracked.

1

Customer walks in · Job is created

Front desk registers customer, logs the device, describes the issue, sets expected delivery. A unique job number is auto-generated and a WhatsApp confirmation is sent.

2

Technician inspects and sends estimate

The assigned technician adds parts, labour, and diagnostic notes. An estimate is sent to the customer via WhatsApp for approval.

3

Customer approves · Work begins

Once approved, the job moves through service. Parts are logged against the job. Every status change is time-stamped and attributed to a staff member.

4

Customer checks status · Customer Portal

The customer gets a secure portal link — no login required. They see real-time job status, technician notes, and parts used, on their phone.

5

Device is ready · WhatsApp alert fires

When the job moves to "Ready for Collection", an automated WhatsApp message is sent. Front desk records final payment and hands over the device.

6

Delivered · Job closes

Job is marked delivered. Payment is reconciled. The full job history — notes, parts, status log, payments — is preserved indefinitely.

Module · Platform Admin

Multi-tenant. One deployment. Many chains.

ServiceDesk is built as a true multi-tenant SaaS — not a single-org tool. The Platform Admin module lets the platform operator onboard and manage multiple service chains from a single control panel.

🛡️
Platform Admin Panel
Operator-only · gated by platform admin email
  • List all organisations on the platform — name, slug, status, created date
  • Onboard a new service chain — create org, first admin, initial branches
  • Activate or suspend an organisation — instant platform-wide effect
  • Impersonate any org for support and troubleshooting
  • Access gated by PLATFORM_ADMIN_EMAIL env var — not a database role
Multi-tenant architecture
  • ✦ Every table has org_id NOT NULL — data never bleeds across tenants
  • ✦ Supabase Row Level Security (RLS) enforces isolation at the database layer — not the app layer
  • ✦ Each org has its own branding, notification config, device fields, and team — fully independent
  • ✦ Single deployment on Vercel serves all tenants — zero per-tenant infrastructure

Lead-to-Job Servicing Flow

  • Overdue alerts: Manager dashboard flags jobs past their estimated delivery date — nothing slips past
  • Payment on delivery: Advance collected at intake, balance recorded at delivery — full payment trail per job
