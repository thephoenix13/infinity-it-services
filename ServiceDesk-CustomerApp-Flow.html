Notifications
Unread highlighted, tap → job

Notifications are sent automatically by the service centre's notification rules. Customers receive them via WhatsApp/SMS and see the same history in-app.

WhatsApp / SMS push

Fires the moment a job status changes. Configured by the service centre — customers don't need to opt in manually.

In-app alert log

Full notification history grouped by date. Unread alerts are highlighted. Tapping any notification deep-links to that job.

Action buttons inline

"View Job →" button inside each alert. For estimate notifications — "Approve" button inline so customer doesn't need to open the full job.

Notification types

Job received · Inspection done · Estimate sent · Approved · In Service · Ready for pickup · Delivered

Feedback
Unlocks after Delivered status

Star Rating

1–5 star tap rating. Required field. Displayed to the service centre admin in their dashboard.

Quick-select tags

Pre-set positive tags (Fast turnaround, Good communication, etc.) — one tap, no typing. Multiple can be selected.

Free-text comment

Optional comment field. Allows the customer to share specific feedback in their own words.

Locked until delivery

Feedback option only appears once the job reaches Delivered status — prevents premature or irrelevant reviews.

Feedback is tied to the job and visible to the admin in their Reports module. Helps identify top-performing technicians and recurring issues.

Grievance
Linked to job, tracked status

Job-linked complaint

Customer selects which job the grievance is about. Ensures the service centre team has full context immediately.

Issue categories

Repair quality · Delay · Parts concern · Billing dispute · Staff behaviour · Other — pre-set dropdown for structured routing.

Grievance tracking

Each grievance gets a unique GRV number. Customer sees status: Open → In Review → Resolved. Full history in-app.

24-hour SLA

Service centre admin is alerted immediately. Committed response within 24 hours — shown to the customer on submission.

Grievances appear in the service centre admin dashboard as a separate queue — separate from job notes, ensuring complaints are never missed.

Profile
History, devices, preferences

Repair summary stats

Total, completed, and active job counts at a glance. Quick health check of the customer's relationship with the shop.

All My Jobs

Full job history across all branches and all devices — same as the Home screen but unfiltered and sortable.

My Devices

All devices the customer has ever brought in — make, model, IMEI/serial. Quickly identify the device when creating a new job.

Notification preferences

Toggle WhatsApp vs. SMS per event type. Customer controls what they receive without involving the service centre.

Profile data syncs with the service centre's customer record — if the customer updates their email, it reflects in the admin CRM automatically.

Next steps

Build it on top of
what's already live.

The service centre platform is fully built. The customer app shares the same Supabase backend — screens can be built as new routes on the existing Next.js app or as a separate PWA pointing to the same API.

Option A — Same App
  • New /customer/* routes in existing Next.js
  • Same Supabase DB, new RLS policies for customers
  • Single deployment on Vercel
Option B — Separate PWA
  • Customer-only app at customer.servicedesk.in
  • Clean separation of concerns
  • Easier to brand per organisation
