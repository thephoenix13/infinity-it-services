Module 2 · Jobs → Job Detail

Everything about one job, one screen.

The job detail page is the central workspace. Every stakeholder — admin, technician, front desk — works here for their part of the job lifecycle.

🔄
Status Transitions
Action buttons at the top move the job forward. Each click opens a modal to add an audit note. Every transition is logged with timestamp + who changed it.
🔩
Parts & Labour
Technicians add parts (name, qty, unit cost) and toggle Labour to mark billable labour time. Totals are calculated automatically.
📝
Notes
Threaded notes with author + timestamp. Technicians log diagnostic findings; managers add customer communication notes.
💳
Payments
Payment panel records advance and final payments. Shows outstanding balance. Front desk uses this when the customer collects the device.

Module 3 · Customers

Every customer, all their history.

Customers are created automatically when a job is raised — no separate signup step. The Customers module is a full searchable directory with job history per customer.

1
Search by name or phone
Type any part of a name or phone number. The list filters client-side instantly — no page reload.
2
Click a customer → full profile
Profile shows name, phone, email, and customer-since date. Below it: every job this customer has ever brought in, with status, category, and expected delivery.
3
Jump to any past job
Click a job number in the customer's history to open that job detail directly. Great for resolving repeat-issue disputes or reviewing repair history.

Demo tip: look up a returning customer by phone at job creation — their details auto-fill in Step 1 of the new job wizard. Show this flow to demonstrate speed at the counter.

Module 7 · Customer Portal

Self-service tracking — no login required.

Every job gets a unique, shareable tracking link. The customer clicks it from WhatsApp or saves it themselves — no account, no password, no app download needed.

1
Find the portal link on any job
Open any job detail page. Scroll to the bottom — the portal link is displayed as a clickable URL. Copy and share via WhatsApp, SMS, or email.
2
Customer opens the link
A clean, mobile-optimised page loads at /track/{token}. It shows the job status, device details, branch contact, and estimated delivery — updated in real time.
3
No login, fully secure
The token in the URL is unique per job. Without that token, the page returns nothing — customers can only see their own job. No cross-customer data exposure.

Demo tip: open a job detail page → copy the /track/… URL → paste in a private browser window. Show how a customer sees it — no auth at all, instant status, clean UI.
