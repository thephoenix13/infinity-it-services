Section 1

Logging In & Roles

ServiceDesk is a web app — open it in any browser on phone, tablet, or desktop. Each staff member logs in with their email and password. What they see depends on their role.

Admin — Full access, settings, all branches
Manager — Reports, team, all jobs in their branch
Front Desk — Create jobs, check-in devices, print invoices
Technician — View assigned jobs, update status, add parts & photos
Login Page All roles
servicedesk-app.vercel.app/login
Enter your email and password. Select your role (Front Desk, Technician, Manager, Admin) from the role cards shown. You are redirected to the dashboard after login.
Branch Picker Admin · Manager
Top bar→Branch dropdown
If your organisation has multiple branches, the top bar shows a branch dropdown. Admins can switch to "All Branches" to see data across the whole organisation.
Forgot Password / Adding New Staff Admin only
New staff accounts are created by the admin under Settings → Team. Provide the staff member's full name, email, role, and branch. They receive a login link by email. Passwords can be reset from the login page using "Forgot password."
Section 2

Front Desk

The Front Desk role handles customer check-ins — creating jobs when a device is brought in, looking up existing customers, and printing invoices when a device is picked up.

Creating a New Job
New Job Form Front Desk
Sidebar→Jobs→New Job
The New Job form is the main check-in screen. Fill in all sections top to bottom and tap "Create Job." The job gets a unique job number automatically (e.g., KTH-0042 for Kothrud branch).
1
Customer details — Enter the customer's name and phone number. If they've visited before, the form auto-fills their profile. Email is optional.
2
Asset / Device — Enter the primary identifier (serial number, IMEI, or any unique ID). Fill in the custom fields your org has set up (brand, model, colour, etc.).
3
Issue details — Select the issue category (e.g., "Screen Damage"), type a description, and check any accessories received (charger, bag, etc.).
4
Photos — Tap the photo area to add inspection photos from your camera or gallery. Document existing damage before work begins.
5
Assignment & delivery — Select the technician to assign the job to, set the expected delivery date, and enter an advance payment if collected.
6
Create Job — Tap the button. The job is created with status "Received". Print or share the job number with the customer.
Managing Jobs
Jobs List Front Desk
Sidebar→Jobs
Shows all jobs for your branch. Filter by status (Received, Ready for Pickup, Overdue, etc.), search by job number, customer name, or phone. Overdue jobs are highlighted in red.
Search by job # or name Filter by status Overdue alert
Job Detail Front Desk
Jobs list→Click job #
Opens the full job card — customer info, device details, issue, parts & labour, photos, timeline, notes, and payments. Front Desk can view all info and record payments.
Print Invoice Front Desk
Job detail→Print Invoice button
Generates a printable invoice showing job details, parts & labour, total amount, payments collected, and balance due. Opens in a new tab — use your browser's print function or save as PDF.
Customer History Front Desk
Sidebar→Customers
Search for any customer by name or phone. Tap a customer to see all their past jobs. Useful for repeat customers to quickly reference previous repairs.
Job Status — What Front Desk Sees
Estimate Approval Front Desk
Job detail→Customer Approval panel
When a technician sends an estimate to the customer, Front Desk can see the approval status on the job detail page. If the customer approves or declines in person (or by phone), Front Desk can record the decision here — "Mark Approved" or "Mark Declined."
Section 3

Technician

Technicians see only the jobs assigned to them. They update the job status as work progresses, add parts and labour used, write diagnosis and resolution notes, and upload photos of the completed repair.

Viewing & Working on Jobs
My Jobs Technician
Sidebar→Jobs
Technicians see all jobs assigned to them across all statuses. Use the status filter to narrow down — e.g., "In Service" to see active work. Overdue jobs are shown in red.
Job Detail Technician
Jobs list→Tap job #
Shows the full job — device info, customer name, issue description, accessories received, parts added, photos, notes, and the complete status timeline.
Job Status Flow
Moving a Job Through Statuses Technician
Job detail→Status buttons (top right)
On the job detail page, blue buttons show the next available status transitions. Tap the button, add an optional note, and confirm. Each status change is logged in the timeline with a timestamp.
Received → Inspection → Estimate Sent → Approved → Waiting Parts → In Service → Ready → Delivered
Note: Moving to "In Service" from "Approved" is only possible after the customer has approved the estimate. If the estimate is still pending, the "In Service" button is locked.
Adding Parts, Labour & Notes
Parts & Labour Technician
Job detail→Parts & Labour card
Enter each part or labour charge — description, quantity, and unit cost. Toggle "Labour" checkbox for service charges. The running total updates automatically and feeds into the invoice.
Parts or labour type Qty + unit cost Auto-totals
Send Estimate Technician
Job detail→Send Estimate panel
When the job is in "Inspection" status and parts are added, the Send Estimate panel appears. Send the estimate to the customer via WhatsApp or SMS. The job moves to "Estimate Sent" status automatically.
Diagnosis Notes Technician
Job detail→Diagnosis Notes card
Document what was found during inspection — the root cause, damage observed, or initial findings. Tap "Add diagnosis notes" or "Edit" to type and save.
Resolution Summary Technician
Job detail→Resolution Summary card
After the repair is done, write what was fixed. This appears on the customer invoice. Tap "Add resolution summary" or "Edit" to fill in.
Internal Notes Technician
Job detail→Notes card
Add timestamped notes visible to all staff on this job — updates, waiting on parts, customer callbacks, etc. Notes are not shown to the customer.
Waiting for Parts Technician
Job detail→→ Waiting Parts button
When transitioning to "Waiting Parts," enter the expected parts arrival date. This is used for overdue alerts — the job will appear as overdue if parts don't arrive by that date.
Photos
Inspection Photos Technician
Job detail→Inspection Photos card
Capture or upload photos showing the device's condition when it was received — cracks, damage, missing parts. On mobile, tap "Add Photos" to open your camera or gallery. Multiple photos allowed.
Post-Service Photos Technician
Job detail→Post-Service Photos card
Once the job is In Service or Ready, upload photos of the completed repair. Documents the work done. The upload button is active only when the job is in the correct stage.

Technician Workflow — End to End

A typical job from assignment to delivery.

1
Technician
Opens Jobs list, finds the new job assigned by Front Desk. Status: Received.
2
Technician
Taps → Inspection. Uploads inspection photos, writes diagnosis notes.
Status moves to Inspection.
3
Technician
Adds parts and labour required. Taps Send Estimate. Customer receives estimate via WhatsApp/SMS.
Status moves to Estimate Sent.
4
Customer / Front Desk
Customer approves estimate via their tracking portal, or Front Desk marks it approved in person.
Status moves to Approved.
5
Technician
Taps → In Service. Performs the repair. Adds resolution summary and post-service photos.
Status moves to In Service. Timer starts.
6
Technician
Taps → Ready. Front Desk is notified that the device is ready for pickup.
Status moves to Ready.
7
Front Desk
Collects final payment, prints invoice, hands device to customer. Marks → Delivered.
Status moves to Delivered. Job closed.
Section 4

Manager / Admin

Managers and Admins have access to reports, technician workload, payments, and all jobs across their branch. Admins additionally control organisation-wide settings and can see data across all branches.

Dashboard
Stat Cards Manager · Admin
Sidebar→Dashboard
Four headline numbers at a glance: Open Jobs, Ready for Pickup, Overdue, and Today's Revenue. Filtered by the selected branch.
Jobs by Status Manager · Admin
Dashboard→Left panel
Horizontal bar chart showing how many jobs are in each status — Received, Inspection, In Service, Ready, etc. Spot bottlenecks instantly.
Quick Actions Manager · Admin
Dashboard→Right panel
One-click links to New Job, View All Jobs, Ready for Pickup, Overdue Jobs, and Reports. Useful as a daily starting point.
Recent Jobs Manager · Admin
Dashboard→Bottom table
Last 10 jobs created in the selected branch — job number, customer, status, and date. Click any row to open the full job detail.
Reports
Overview Tab Manager · Admin
Sidebar→Reports→Overview
Total jobs, completed jobs, revenue, and average job value for the selected period (7, 30, or 90 days). Revenue trend bar chart and breakdown by category and technician.
Overdue Tab Manager · Admin
Reports→Overdue
Full list of jobs past their expected delivery date. Shows customer, branch, technician, status, due date, and how many days overdue. Export to CSV available.
Revenue Tab Admin only
Reports→Revenue
Daily revenue breakdown with totals. Shows revenue by branch across the organisation. Useful for tracking which branches are performing.
Margin Tab Admin only
Reports→Margin
Revenue, parts cost, labour cost, gross margin, and margin % by branch. Helps identify where the shop is most profitable.
Customers Tab Manager · Admin
Reports→Customers
Customer return rate — how many customers have come back for a second job. Shows total customers vs. repeat customers for the selected period.
Branches Tab Admin only
Reports→Branches
Side-by-side comparison of all branches — total jobs, revenue, avg job value, and on-time delivery rate. Exportable to CSV.
Print / Export: All report pages have a "🖨 Print / PDF" button at the top. For tabular sections, "Export CSV" downloads the data as a spreadsheet.
Technician Workload
Technician Workload View Manager · Admin
Sidebar→Technicians
One card per technician showing: number of open jobs, how many are overdue, and a breakdown by status (Inspection, In Service, Ready, etc.). Tap "View all jobs" on any card to open the full job list filtered to that technician. Use this to reassign work when a technician is overloaded.
Payments
Recording Payments Manager · Admin
Job detail→Payments card
Record payments collected at any point — advance at check-in or final at delivery. Enter amount and payment method (cash, UPI, card, etc.). The balance due is calculated automatically from parts total minus payments.
Advance Payment Front Desk
New Job form→Advance Payment field
Advance collected at check-in can be entered directly in the New Job form. It is recorded as the first payment on the job automatically.
Warranty
Setting a Warranty Manager · Admin
Job detail→Warranty card (visible after Delivered)
Once a job is delivered, a warranty expiry date can be set on the job. The job detail page shows a green "Warranty valid until" badge when active, and a grey "Warranty expired" badge after the date passes. Useful for honouring repair warranties on repeat visits.
Section 5

Customer Tracking Portal

Every job gets a unique tracking link that can be shared with the customer. They can check their job status in real time — no app download, no login required.

Tracking Link Customer-facing
Job detail→Customer tracking link
The tracking URL (e.g., servicedesk-app.vercel.app/track/abc123) is shown at the bottom of every job detail page. Share it with the customer via WhatsApp or SMS. They can bookmark it and check status any time.
What the Customer Sees Customer-facing
Customer's phone browser
The portal shows: current status, device identifier, estimated delivery date, technician name, and a status timeline. It does not show internal notes or pricing details until an estimate is sent.
Estimate Approval Customer-facing
Customer tracking portal→Approve / Decline buttons
When an estimate is sent, the customer sees the itemised parts and labour list with the total cost on their tracking page. They can tap "Approve" to let the technician proceed, or "Decline" and provide a reason. The job status updates immediately and the technician/front desk is notified.
Offline approval: If the customer approves in person or over the phone, Front Desk or the Manager can mark the approval manually from the job detail page under the "Customer Approval" panel.
Section 6

Settings & Configuration

Settings are accessible only to Admins. Configure your organisation profile, branches, service categories, team members, and notifications from one place.

Organisation & Branches
Organisation Settings Admin only
Settings→Organisation
Set your shop name and address. This appears on printed invoices and the customer tracking portal.
Branches Admin only
Settings→Branches
Add or edit branches. Each branch has a name, a 2–4 letter prefix (used in job numbers, e.g., "KTH"), address, and phone. The phone is shown on the estimate sent to the customer.
Branch name Job # prefix Phone & address
Team Management
Adding Team Members Admin only
Settings→Team→Add member
Enter full name, email, role, and branch assignment. The new member receives a login email. To deactivate someone, use the disable toggle on their row. Roles can be changed at any time.
1
Full name + email — Login credentials are sent to this email.
2
Role — Front Desk, Technician, Manager, or Admin.
3
Branch — Which branch they belong to. Admins and Managers can be assigned to all branches.
Service Categories & Branding
Service Categories Admin only
Settings→Categories
Define the issue categories shown in the New Job form — e.g., "Screen Damage," "Battery Replacement," "Water Damage." Add, edit, or remove as your service offering changes.
Branding Admin only
Settings→Branding
Upload your shop logo and set your brand colour. The logo appears on the customer portal and printed invoices. The brand colour is applied to buttons and accents throughout the app.
Notifications
WhatsApp / SMS Notifications Admin only
Settings→Notifications
Connect a WhatsApp or SMS provider to send customers automated messages at key job milestones — when an estimate is ready, when the repair is complete, and when the device is ready for pickup. Configure your provider API keys here and toggle which events trigger a message.
Estimate sent Job ready for pickup Job delivered WhatsApp or SMS
Asset fields: The "Asset" section of the New Job form shows custom fields (brand, model, colour, etc.) that are configured under Settings → Organisation. Contact your admin to add or remove asset fields.
